Test-only increment after `3a7bd426`; all six issues remain incomplete.

The maintained delayed-commit process test now has two schedules. The original schedule still proves a fresh adapter can read0 before the old delayed remote mutation commits, then read1 afterward, without replay. The new schedule dispatches a fresh read with ID1 after the original write's adapter was killed and its private TLS connection closed. That fresh read reaches a fixture gate and remains held while the original ID1 write commits and attempts its terminal response on the old closed connection.

Only after observing the old terminal send attempt does the fixture release the fresh read. The fresh adapter must receive its exact read result for ID1 and then a successful continued ID2 read. A schema-owned operation discriminator distinguishes `fixture_read` from `fixture_increment`, preventing a stale write response from passing merely because both responses contain writes1. All fresh stdout bytes are collected through actual process close and parsed: exactly ID1/read and ID2/read may appear, without an extra old terminal. The original stdout remains empty. Final counters require one write dispatch, two reads and no fixture handler failures.
